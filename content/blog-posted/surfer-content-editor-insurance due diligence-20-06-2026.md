### What insurance areas can your experts cover?

Experts cover general liability, property, workers compensation, D&O, E&O, cyber, environmental liability, captive insurance, reinsurance arrangements, and specialty coverage. They can assess tail coverage needs for claims-made policies, review change of control provisions, evaluate emerging risks like climate and ESG exposure, and help structure post-close coverage for successful insurance integration.

A comprehensive document portfolio should include historical insurance policies and claims. Your experts can tell you what to request and what the documents mean.

### Can experts help with specific deal questions?

Yes. Insurance due diligence can reveal gaps in coverage that increase risk. Experts can assess whether coverage limits match asset values, whether insurance warranties in the purchase agreement are accurate, whether carve outs create exposure, and whether reviewing changes in coverage indicates financial stress or high risk at the target. Legal and regulatory exposure should be examined for pending or historical litigation, and your experts can flag where to look.

Loss and claims history should be examined to identify trends and outstanding liabilities. Evaluating claims history highlights recurring issues and potential exposures. Contracts should verify liability assumptions and required coverage limits. These are the questions your experts answer.
