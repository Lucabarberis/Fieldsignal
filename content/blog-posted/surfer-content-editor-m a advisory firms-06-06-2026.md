# M&A Advisory Firms: A Comparison Guide

M&A advisory firms provide strategic guidance on acquisitions and mergers, facilitate negotiations to achieve favorable deal terms, and prevent you from undervaluing your business. Choosing the right advisory firm directly impacts the valuation and success of a deal.

The market for mergers and acquisitions advisory is broad. At the top, investment banking giants like J.P. Morgan (with $158.1 billion in revenue in 2023), Morgan Stanley ($111 billion in deal value in 2023), and Citigroup ($34 billion in deal value in 2023) handle massive corporate transactions. Wells Fargo advised on $45 billion in deals for H1 2024 alone. Below them, boutique advisory firms provide specialized industry advice and personalized attention due to their smaller size. These boutique firms typically charge a success fee upon deal closure, and their expertise benefits industries outside traditional investment banking. Then there are the big four accounting firms, law firms, and expert networks that round out the advisory team.

What makes one advisor better than another? Deal size fit, fee transparency, senior partner involvement, and buyer network access. This guide breaks down seven firms worth evaluating.

## How We Chose the Best M&A Advisory Firms

A strong deal track record is crucial when selecting an M&A advisory firm. Evaluating any advisory firm involves assessing deal size compatibility and firm scale. Here's what we weighted:

1. **Deal size focus.** Does the firm actively work your deal range, or are you a rounding error?

2. **Pricing transparency.** M&A advisors typically charge retainers plus success fees based on deal size. We favored firms with clear fee structures and aligned incentives with clients.

3. **Industry expertise.** Industry-specific expertise helps advisors identify unique opportunities and attract the right buyers.

4. **Speed and process.** Lower middle market deals close in 6 to 9 months. Delays cost money.

5. **Senior involvement.** Who runs your deal day to day?

6. **Compliance.** Regulatory advisory involves handling antitrust concerns and competition laws. Firms need the infrastructure for it.

7. **Buyer network.** Firms identify potential buyers or acquisition targets using extensive networks. Advisory firms also assist in business valuations to determine fair value, which matters when you're running a competitive process.

M&A advisors help maximize transaction value and minimize operational risk. The firms below do that in different ways.

Goldman's brand signals credibility to buyers and sellers. When you're running a confidential sale above $500 million, that signal matters. They bring strategic advice, financing relationships, and deal execution capabilities that few can match.

Public company takeovers, cross border mergers, multibillion-dollar strategic acquisitions, and private equity transactions requiring financial performance credibility. Goldman helps analyze market trends for clients pursuing joint ventures and notable deals.

## How to Choose the Right M&A Advisory Firm

### Choose Based on Deal Size

Deal value determines which firms will even take your call. Goldman and Houlihan won't staff a $10M deal. Generational Equity and Benchmark won't run a $5B deal. In the lower middle market ($5M to $100M), boutique firms and specialized sell side shops deliver more value per dollar. U.S. PE middle market deal value hit roughly [$410.7 billion in 2025](https://procloser.ai/blog/best-ma-advisors-mid-sized-companies/?utm_source=openai), so there's no shortage of advisors competing for your mandate. Typical success fees run 3 to 8% in this range.

### Choose Based on Geographic Scope

If your buyer pool includes government agencies, large corporations, or financial sponsors outside your home market, you need global reach. Deloitte, Benchmark, and Clairfield all offer cross-border expertise. For domestic-only deals in San Francisco or other U.S. markets, a regional boutique advisory firm with local relationships often outperforms.

### Choose Based on Industry Specialization

An advisor who knows life sciences won't waste time pitching your company to irrelevant buyers. Sector expertise directly impacts buyer identification, valuation multiples, and financial performance benchmarks used in pricing. The best acquisitions consulting firms match their advisory team's expertise to your industry.
