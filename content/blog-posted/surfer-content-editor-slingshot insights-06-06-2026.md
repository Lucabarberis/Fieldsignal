# Slingshot Insights: Expert Network Profile and Alternatives

Slingshot Insights is an expert network that makes primary research more affordable by letting members share costs for expert interviews and surveys. It's strongest when you need healthcare, biotech, or sector research with transcripts, shared expert calls, and lower-cost access than traditional enterprise expert networks.

If you're comparing Slingshot against GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, Guidepoint, Tegus, AlphaSense, Capvision, ProSapient, Coleman Research, Atheneum, Mosaic Research Management, Inex One, or FieldSignal, the key question is simple: do you need shared research at a lower price, or private custom research with faster control?

## What Is Slingshot Insights

Slingshot Insights is a New York-based expert network founded in 2013. The company is known for a crowdfunding model that lets members collaborate on research projects, propose interview topics, suggest questions, and split the cost of expert calls.

The company was created by co founders Joe McCann, a former healthcare analyst at CR Intrinsic, part of SAC Capital, and Colleen Tepe, an ex-management consultant with Fortune 500 experience. That background explains why Slingshot first gained traction with investors, individual investors, biotech-focused funds, consultants, and strategic teams that needed primary market intelligence without GLG-tier retainers.

Slingshot started with a strong healthcare and biotech focus, which made it useful for buyers searching for a healthcare survey firm or expert interviews with physicians, KOLs, commercial leaders, and other healthcare experts. Slingshot Insights expanded to all sectors through the acquisition of Noble Insights, including technology, industrials, financial services, consumer goods, and energy.

The platform allows members to collaborate on research projects. The community collaborates to track critical industry trends. Slingshot reports over 25,000 expert calls executed for industry insights, 2,000+ transcripts available for analyzing industry trends, and 200+ institutional firms utilize expert insights for strategy.

Slingshot also reports over 25,000 expert calls executed by Slingshot Insights. Slingshot Insights offers 2,000+ searchable transcripts and surveys. 200+ institutional firms trust Slingshot Insights for research.

## How Slingshot Insights Works

Slingshot works differently from traditional expert networks. Instead of every client commissioning a private call at full cost, Slingshot lets members create research projects, recruit other members into the same topic, and share the project budget.

Members can request expert interviews, create surveys, propose interview topics, submit questions, vote on questions, and access recordings or transcripts after the call. Members can share costs for expert interviews and surveys, and the cost of conducting expert interviews can be reduced by up to 80% when enough participants join.

The basic workflow looks like this:

1. **A member proposes a topic.**\
   A user starts with a sector, company, product, regulatory event, or market question.

2. **Slingshot scopes the project.**\
   The team reviews the request, confirms the desired expert profile, and sets a budget.

3. **Other members join.**\
   The project gains demand from the community. As more members join, sharing lowers the per-member cost.

4. **Experts are sourced and vetted.**\
   Slingshot recruits industry professionals, Key Opinion Leaders, executives, practitioners, or other relevant experts.

5. **The call or research project is completed.**\
   Members can attend live, listen later, read transcripts, or use the library for continuing research.

6. **Compliance records are retained.**\
   Calls are recorded and transcribed, which creates a clear audit trail.

The platform provides external compliance tools for institutional clients. Slingshot also requires compliance attestations, prohibits material nonpublic information, records calls, transcribes calls, and gives clients a way to flag issues.

Typical deliverables include:

* Expert calls

* Expert interviews

* Recorded calls

* Transcripts

* Surveys

* AI-generated summaries where available

* Management meeting access

* Searchable library access

The platform has a large repository of recorded expert calls. Over 25,000 expert calls executed. 2,000+ transcripts available for expert consultations. Trusted by 200+ institutional firms for expert insights.

### Expert Interview Process

Booking experts through Slingshot is structured around a shared research request.

1. **Create or join a project.**\
   You can create a new expert call topic or join an existing project already open to members.

2. **Define the expert profile.**\
   You specify the industry, company type, role, region, and experience needed.

3. **Submit questions.**\
   Users can propose interview topics and suggest questions. Other members can respond by voting on questions they want the Call Leader to ask.

4. **Slingshot sources experts.**\
   The team identifies relevant industry professionals and works to confirm fit before the call.

5. **The call takes place.**\
   Calls are usually conducted by phone or video. In-person meetings may be possible when scoped as a management meeting or custom engagement.

6. **You receive the transcript.**\
   Members can read the transcript, review the recording, and use the output in diligence, strategy work, or market validation.

This works well when the topic has enough member demand. It works less well when you need a fully private call, niche expert recruitment, or immediate access without waiting for a shared project to fill.

### Research Project Structure

Slingshot also supports research projects beyond one expert call. Expert surveys provide structured data from industry practitioners, which is useful when you need directional market data rather than one person’s opinion.

Common project types include:

* Single expert interviews

* Group expert calls

* Multi-expert surveys

* Healthcare surveys

* Management meetings

* Transcript library research

* Custom research projects tied to sector trends

Timeline depends on the scope. A library search can be fast if relevant transcripts already exist. A standard expert call requires expert matching, member participation, scheduling, and compliance review. A multi-expert survey or panel takes longer because Slingshot has to recruit enough qualified practitioners and verify the sample.

For a PE associate, VC analyst, consultant, or founder, the tradeoff is clear: Slingshot gives you cost sharing and transcript access, but you may give up speed and full control over questions unless you’re the Call Leader.

### How does Slingshot's crowdfunding model work?

Slingshot’s crowdfunding model lets members create research projects around a topic, such as a company, sector, product launch, reimbursement issue, technology trend, or investment question.

Members then join the project and share the budget. As more members join, the cost per member declines. Members can suggest questions, vote on questions, listen live, or read the transcript after the expert call.

If the project doesn’t receive enough participation, the call may not happen or may be delayed. That’s the main timeline tradeoff. You get lower cost through sharing, but you may lose speed and control.
