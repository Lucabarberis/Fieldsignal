## What actually affects your AlphaSights pay

Your title alone doesn't set your rate. Here's what actually moves the number:

* **Seniority and recency.** Recent, hands-on operators in 2024 to 2026 roles get better alphasights consultant pay than professionals who left an industry a decade ago. Recency is the single biggest lever.

* **Specialization.** Narrow domains (US specialty pharmacy reimbursement, EU SaaS procurement in manufacturing, LATAM B2B fintech) often support a high rate of $500+ per hour because there are fewer credible industry experts.

* **Client type and urgency.** A pre-LOI private equity deal with a tight IC date typically tolerates higher rates than a slow-burn corporate strategy project. Alphasights clients in the investment space are often willing to pay more.

* **Project scope and prep.** Calls that require pre-reading, data pulls, or follow-up work can justify higher compensation than low-prep background conversations.

* **Past client feedback.** Strong post-call ratings on clarity, candor, and commercial insight increase your chances of repeat invites at your chosen rate.

* **Compliance comfort.** AlphaSights operates under a compliance framework for expert consultations. Clients favor experts who understand confidentiality boundaries, what constitutes confidential information, and what they cannot say about their employer or former employer. This makes those experts "safer" and more likely to be rebooked.

AlphaSights, like other networks, will sometimes push back on very high first-time rates. An associate may suggest a band that fits current demand, so there's always a negotiate step in the process.

## How the AlphaSights consulting process works for experts

Here's exactly what happens, step by step, before you get paid as an alphasights expert.

1. **Outreach and screening.** AlphaSights typically finds experts via LinkedIn outreach or referrals. A team member will run quick fit and conflict checks to identify whether you're a match. AlphaSights was founded in 2008 by Andrew Heath and Max Cartellieri and now has nine offices in major cities worldwide, including San Francisco, New York, and London.

2. **Profile and rate setting.** You'll provide a CV-style profile and propose an hourly rate. AlphaSights may benchmark your rate against similar experts to determine fit.

3. **Project invitation.** You'll receive an email describing the project: client type (e.g., US middle-market PE fund), target company, sector, and 3 to 6 key questions. Most opportunities come with enough context to decide quickly.

4. **Compliance signoff.** You'll confirm you're not restricted (e.g., not a current insider at a public company) and that you understand not to share MNPI or confidential data. This is where compliance and confidentiality requirements are enforced.

5. **Scheduling and execution.** Common formats are 45 to 60 minute calls, sometimes longer 90-minute sessions, usually on Zoom or phone. The conversation is structured around the client's research questions.

6. **Payment.** AlphaSights pays based on call duration at the agreed rate, typically within a few business days. You're responsible for your own tax reporting.

You have full discretion to accept or decline each project. Project flow is often irregular. Some weeks you'll get five invitations, other weeks zero. Most experts refer to this as the "feast or famine" pattern.

Here's how to pick a number and win recurring work instead of chasing the highest headline rate.
