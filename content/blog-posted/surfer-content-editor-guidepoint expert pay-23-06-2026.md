## How Guidepoint consulting rates are actually set

Guidepoint advisors propose an hourly rate when setting up their profile. But your final pay depends on a paid consulting request from a client, your profile match, and how you respond to screening questions.

Factors that influence your rate:

* **Seniority and years in role.** More experience means more bargaining power. Firsthand decision-level experience is highly valued.

* **Company brand.** Having worked at well-known companies matters to most clients.

* **Domain rarity.** AI infra experts are scarcer than general business ops professionals. Rare profiles get paid more.

* **Geography.** Location can influence compensation for experts. U.S. and Western European experts typically command higher rates.

* **Supply and demand.** If many experts match a client request, rates get driven down. Engagement volume can affect high hourly rates for expert consultations.

Advisors are paid based on their customary consulting fees agreed with Guidepoint. * Never share material non-public information or confidential information from a current employer or very recent role.

* Proactively disclose potential conflicts to your Guidepoint coordinator before calls. Say "I can't discuss that" when clients push into restricted territory.

**Unsafe topics:** live M&A deals, non-public clinical data, internal regulatory strategy from your current employer, confidential information covered by active NDAs.
