### Step 1: Optimize your LinkedIn profile for expert network searches

AlphaSights prefers experts to have a strong LinkedIn profile. Their associates search LinkedIn by keyword, so your profile needs to identify your niche clearly.

* Make your headline specific. "Senior Product Manager, B2B SaaS billing, ex-Stripe" beats "Product Leader."

* Add detailed role descriptions with company names, regions, and segments. Think "US SMB solar installers" or "GTM for Hong Kong enterprise banking."

* Write a short About section listing 3 to 5 topics you can credibly speak on for 30 to 60 minutes.

* Include a non-corporate email in your Contact section. Expert networks prefer direct email over InMail.

* Add phrases like "available for expert network consulting" so you appear in searches from AlphaSights, GLG, Third Bridge, Guidepoint, and boutique firms like FieldSignal.

Your LinkedIn profile should read like a set of job descriptions for the expertise you're selling, not a resume.

AlphaSights' client service team pays close attention to feedback. Early performance determines how often you're invited back.

AlphaSights is a legitimate expert network serving professional investors, consultants, and corporates. It's not a pyramid scheme.
