## Deal Success Through Expert Insights

The best expert calls don't just confirm what you already believe. They challenge the investment thesis before capital is at risk.

A typical example: a mid-market PE team evaluates a vertical software company with strong reported retention and attractive expansion revenue. Management presents the company's business model as durable, and the financials support a positive story. Before signing an LOI, the deal team runs calls with former customers and channel partners.

The calls surface three issues:

1. Several customers are dissatisfied with the product roadmap.

2. New competitors are bundling similar features into lower-priced platforms.

3. Implementation delays are increasing churn risk in the customer base.

Those expert insights don't automatically kill the deal. They change the investment decision. The private equity team can revise the model, adjust the valuation, negotiate stronger protections, or walk away before spending more diligence budget.

* **IC preparation**: Support investment decisions with expert transcripts and structured summaries.

* **Post investment**: Inform value creation plans, hiring priorities, customer strategy, pricing moves, and new markets.
