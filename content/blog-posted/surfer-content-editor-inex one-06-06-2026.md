## How Inex One Works

Inex One streamlines market research and due diligence for professionals through a straightforward aggregation model, and users often describe the workflow improvement as a **game changer** for expert research speed and coordination.

You post a project request on the platform. More than 30 expert networks receive that request and propose relevant experts. You review profiles, compare options, and book expert calls or surveys, all within one interface. Digital platforms like this can reduce administrative hassle and lead times for research significantly.

The platform includes AI-powered transcript tools. Every expert call gets transcribed within approximately 15 minutes, and you receive an AI-generated summary automatically. AI-generated transcript summaries reduce manual note-taking time for analysts. There's also an LLM-based chat tool that lets you query across your transcript library, pull specific quotes, and extract key findings. The AI only returns content from your selected transcripts, so it won't hallucinate external information. AI tools can analyze insights across past research projects, making it easier to spot patterns across multiple expert interviews.

Unified billing and project management tie it all together. A pay-per-use model provides a single invoice for projects across multiple vendors. You manage scheduling, communication, and quote comparisons inside the platform instead of through email chains. For surveys, you can issue RFQs to multiple survey firms and compare bids on cost, sample size, and turnaround, all within the same interface. Digital platforms enable users to compare quotes from multiple survey firms, which removes a lot of back-and-forth.

Centralized project management helps in coordinating expert calls and vendor bids without switching between tools. Building a searchable library of transcripts prevents redundant interviews, so your team doesn't pay twice for the same insight.
