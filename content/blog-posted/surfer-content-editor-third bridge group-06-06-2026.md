# Third Bridge Group: Company Profile, Services, and Pricing

Third Bridge is a global expert network founded in 2007 in London. It connects investors, strategic consulting firms, and corporate strategy teams with industry experts to inform investment decisions and due diligence. It's one of the "big five" expert networks in an industry valued at approximately $2 billion annually. ## What Is Third Bridge Group

Third Bridge (originally named Cognolink) was established in 2007 by three former Bain & Company consultants. The company rebranded in 2015 and has since expanded its global footprint significantly. It's a leading global investment research firm that specializes in connecting clients with industry professionals to evaluate investment opportunities and enhance decision making.

The bridge group's core business model goes beyond simple expert access. Third Bridge distinguishes itself by focusing on qualitative expert-driven insights, layering in-house analysts who moderate calls, apply structured interview frameworks, and build a content library covering tens of thousands of companies. The mission is to democratize human insights for better decisions, fueling decisions across private equity, hedge funds, mutual funds, and corporate strategy teams.

Third Bridge has a team of 1,000+ employees globally, with offices in London, New York, and several other locations across multiple continents. The company serves over 1,000 clients including private equity funds and hedge funds. Third Bridge connects clients with over 1,000 industry specialists and provides insights across a wide range of sectors and geographies.

## Third Bridge's Core Services

Third Bridge offers several interlocking services designed to deliver human led insights at scale:

* **Expert Consultation Calls (Connections).** One-on-one calls with vetted subject matter experts, moderated by Third Bridge analysts. These aren't client-led Q&A sessions. Analysts guide the conversation using structured agendas, probe for depth, and ensure consistency. You get recordings, transcripts, and AI summaries. Third Bridge conducts over 10,000 interviews each year.

* **Transcript Library.** A searchable repository covering over 65,000 companies. Third Bridge maintains a specialized content library of insights from prior interviews. Clients can access 15,000+ previous interview transcripts on demand without scheduling a single call. This is useful when you need to hear perspectives on a company or sector quickly, drawing on the world's human insights already captured.

* **Maps.** Visual value-chain and industry structure tools covering over 100,000 companies. These help you identify competitive positions, supply chains, and market dynamics for both public and private companies.

* **Surveys.** Custom B2B surveys managed by Third Bridge research teams. These cover customer feedback, competitor benchmarking, and pricing studies. Useful for data you can't get from a single expert interaction.

* **Advisors.** Long-term advisory relationships with senior industry experts. More than a one-off consultation. These are embedded experts for ongoing strategic support, ideal for portfolio companies or business leaders managing a forward-looking strategy.

Third Bridge also offers data feeds and AI integration, including partnerships with platforms like Claude (Anthropic), Aiera, Hebbia, and Rogo. Their Bridge MCP (Model Context Protocol) allows transcript access directly within LLMs, enabling AI-powered search and summaries of their expert insights archive.

## How Third Bridge Works

### Client Project Initiation

When you engage Third Bridge, the process starts with project scoping. You define the sector, geography, expertise level, and type of insight you need, whether that's due diligence, competitive positioning, or technology scouting. Third Bridge analysts work with your team to map key themes and hypotheses, then custom-source experts based on those definitions.

Compliance screening is built into every step. Third Bridge emphasizes robust compliance processes to protect clients and experts. Every expert undergoes background checks and must certify no material nonpublic information will be disclosed. Self-referrals are prohibited. Specialists must adhere to strict compliance standards, and Third Bridge's compliance program has three key pillars. Clients can also overlay their own compliance rules, such as restricted lists.

### Expert Matching and Scheduling

Experts are recruited specifically for each project unless existing matches are available. Matching considers relevance, geography, language, and seniority. Because Third Bridge doesn't rely on open self-signups, matching tends to produce higher signal quality. The trade-off is time. Standard domains often connect within 24-48 hours. Niche or senior experts may take longer.

You review proposed expert profiles and select who you want to speak with before any call is scheduled.

### Call Execution and Follow-up

Calls are moderated by Third Bridge analysts, who guide the conversation, probe deeper than surface answers, and maintain compliance monitoring throughout. Advisors cannot disclose confidential information during calls.

After the call, you receive transcripts (often multilingual), summaries, and key takeaways. For Library content, interviews are reviewed and eventually published, creating a compounding content asset. Expert consultations help businesses synthesize vast amounts of data quickly, turning a single conversation into a reusable research artifact.

## Third Bridge Pricing Structure

Third Bridge doesn't publish pricing. Here's what public sources and client reports indicate:

* **Credit-based model.** Clients purchase bundles of credits. A standard expert call consumes a set number of credits. Senior or highly specialized experts require credit multipliers, increasing your effective cost per call.

* **Expert hourly rates.** For technology or highly technical domains, experts often charge $300 to $1,200+ per hour depending on seniority and specialization. Third Bridge applies its own markup on top of the expert's hourly rate.

* **Annual retainers.** Institutional subscription clients tend to start at $50,000+ per year. Enterprise agreements often include minimum commitments and may bundle Library access, Maps, and a set number of credits.

* **Hidden cost factors.** Fast turnaround requests, rare geographies, and heavy compliance filtering all increase costs. The effective cost per call often surprises new users because senior expert multipliers and overhead aren't always transparent upfront.

For mid-market firms or smaller funds, Third Bridge's pricing can feel opaque. Minimum thresholds and subscription requirements aren't always disclosed until you're deep into the sales process. Expert networks charge significant fees for valuable strategic intel, and Third Bridge sits at the premium end of that spectrum.
