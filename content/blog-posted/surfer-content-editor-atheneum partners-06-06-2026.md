## How Atheneum’s expert network model works

Atheneum sources current and former operators, executives, managers, customers, suppliers, and specialists for 1-hour consulting calls and custom research. Atheneum connects clients with vetted industry experts through a rigorous verification process for its experts.

The process is simple:

1. You send a brief with company, industry, region, and target person.

2. Atheneum runs expert recruitment and returns relevant profiles.

3. You pick experts, schedule calls, and run the consultation.

4. Atheneum can add surveys, moderation, synthesis of interviews, and custom reports.

Atheneum's expert recruitment process uses vertical teams in healthcare, technology, industrials, and finance. Its matching may use machine learning \[VERIFY\], but human project teams handle screening, compliance, and quality review.

Use cases include market entry, commercial due diligence, product testing, competitive analysis, industry trend analysis, and support for clients decision making.
