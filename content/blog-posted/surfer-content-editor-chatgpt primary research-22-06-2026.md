# ChatGPT Primary Research: A Practical Workflow

ChatGPT is a planning tool, not a data source. It's useful for framing questions, scanning publicly available information, and drafting research instruments. It won't replace interviews with former operators, structured surveys, or compliance-vetted expert calls. Here's a workflow that combines both.

## Answer First: When ChatGPT Is (and Isn't) Enough for Primary Research

Primary research involves collecting new original data specifically for your study. ChatGPT doesn't do that. It generates text based on patterns in its training data. It can't call a former VP of Product, survey 200 enterprise buyers, or tell you what a company's churn looked like last quarter.

For serious market research, investment memos, or M&A work, treat ChatGPT as a planning and synthesis tool. Some teams still rely on ChatGPT alone for planning, but FieldSignal or a similar expert network is the actual source for original data. Primary research with ChatGPT involves assisting in creating or analyzing original data, not originating it.

ChatGPT is best for early-stage idea exploration, not final research. Maintain human control over data collection while using AI for research.

Typical use cases for you: scoping a pre-IC memo, building a pre-diligence thesis, or doing early product validation as a founder.

**Use ChatGPT for:**

* Framing research questions and drafting hypotheses

* Summarizing public reports, filings, and secondary sources

* Designing interview guides and survey instruments

* Coding and synthesizing transcripts after expert calls

* Creating detailed buyer personas for your target business

**Don't use ChatGPT for:**

* Generating original field data (customer interviews, expert panels)

* Verifying regulatory rules, recent financials, or customer behavior

* Fabricating statistics or survey results for pitch decks

* Simulating expert interviews and treating them as real evidence

## What "Primary Research" Means in a ChatGPT World

Primary research is new data collected directly from people or organizations: interviews, surveys, panels, observations. Secondary research is synthesizing what already exists: reports, articles, research papers, public filings.

ChatGPT models existing text data and patterns. By default, it's a secondary research helper. ChatGPT can gather publicly available market data quickly, and you can use deep research mode in ChatGPT for extensive topic exploration. But it doesn't originate new field data. ChatGPT's information may be outdated or not current, depending on the model version and its training cutoff.

**Terminology for the target reader:**

* Market research: a mix of primary and secondary methods to assess demand, pricing, and competition.

* User research: behavior and preference data from end users, almost always requiring direct observation or conversation.

* Competitive intelligence: insights from former employees, suppliers, and customers about strategy, product roadmap, and pricing.

* Deep research: combining primary and secondary sources for rich, original insight, common in investment and consulting contexts.

**Myth vs Reality:**

| Myth                                                            | Reality                                                                         |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ChatGPT can "do" primary research by simulating interviews      | It generates plausible text based on patterns. Simulations aren't evidence.     |
| ChatGPT always cites sources accurately                         | It can hallucinate citations or misrepresent content.                           |
| ChatGPT can verify regulatory timelines and financial specifics | These need external verification through expert interviews or official filings. |

## Designing ChatGPT Prompts for Serious Market Research

Vague prompts produce shallow output. When prompting ChatGPT for serious market research, precision matters. Effective prompts should specify exact information needed: geography, segment, time horizon, metrics, and format.

Role-playing as an expert improves ChatGPT's market research outputs. Researchers can study how LLMs respond to specific prompts and iterate from there.

**Example prompt patterns:**

1. "Act as a sector analyst specializing in payments regulation in the EU and U.S., with five years advising fintech SaaS companies. Identify regulatory trends from 2025 to 2026, enforcement status, likely changes, and downstream effects on unit economics."

2. "Act as a buy-side associate evaluating a mid-market SaaS payments company with $10 to $30M ARR operating in both the U.S. and Germany. What benchmarks for CAC, churn, ACV, and gross margin are expected based on recent transactions?"

3. "Act as a product manager launching into the EU in late 2026. What product features are table stakes for compliance and localization, and what features differentiate premium offerings?"

Follow-up prompts can refine ChatGPT's responses for better results. You can also ask for workflow suggestions across common apps when planning research tasks. Ask for a table, then drill into a specific row. Ask for a risk matrix, then request the top three by probability.

ChatGPT helps identify customer demographics quickly, and it can create detailed buyer personas for businesses. Buyer personas include demographic and psychographic information. Effective buyer personas inform marketing strategies and product development. You can generate them for your target audience by specifying company stage, revenue band, and decision-maker persona.

**Generic vs improved prompts:**

| Too generic                                         | Improved                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| "Give me metrics for SaaS payments companies."      | "For U.S. mid-market B2B payments SaaS with $5 to $20M ARR, what are typical 2025 CAC payback periods, net dollar retention, and gross margin benchmarks?"                            |

## Using ChatGPT to Plan, Not Fake, Primary Data Collection

You can use ChatGPT to draft interview guides with 8 to 12 question blocks per audience. For former SaaS product executives: pricing evolution, product roadmap trade-offs, technical debt, regulation challenges. For current users: value received, satisfaction, unmet needs, feature prioritization, churn drivers. For competitors' customers: switching triggers, purchase decision criteria, support satisfaction, feature gaps.

For survey question banks, ask ChatGPT to produce items measuring NPS, churn reasons (cost, features, lack of integrations), willingness to pay across pricing tiers, and feature prioritization using rank-choice and rating scales. Simulate interviews to test questionnaire structures using ChatGPT before going live.

AI should not fabricate raw primary data and requires verification. Never ask ChatGPT to "invent survey results" or "simulate 50 customer interviews" for investment-grade work. That invalidates rigor and creates compliance risk.

## From Raw Interviews to Insights: ChatGPT as an Analysis Assistant

After you've run 5 to 20 expert calls, here's the workflow.

1. Store transcripts as text. Redact PII, deal names, and employer specifics.

2. Highlight key quotes manually or with a tool. Organize by pre-identified themes.

3. Feed qualitative data to ChatGPT for thematic analysis and sentiment scoring.

4. Use ChatGPT to summarize qualitative data and identify key themes.

**Concrete analysis tasks:**

* Generate thematic codes across all interviews (compliance cost, feature gaps, competitive response, pricing pressure).

* Summarize each interview in 10 bullet points.

* Pull verbatim evidence under each theme for your report.

* Draft a two-page executive summary with a comparison table: U.S. vs EU, present risk vs future risk.

Note: ChatGPT themes should support, not replace, manual review of transcripts.

## Limitations: Where ChatGPT Breaks for Primary Research

ChatGPT can generate inaccurate or fabricated information. It hallucinates sources, invents statistics, and doesn't have access to non-public calls, panels, or paywalled research papers. Users must verify ChatGPT's outputs before publication. ChatGPT's responses may lack depth and comprehensiveness for specialized topics.

**Three concrete failure modes:**

1. Invented statistics: ChatGPT might claim "CAC dropped 30% in 2025 for EU payment SaaS" with no basis. In reality, the average customer acquisition cost for U.S. checking accounts was $293 in 2019, and the average U.S. customer acquisition cost rose from $437 to $743 from 2019 to 2022. Direct mail acquisition costs exceeded $1,000 in H1 2022. These are verifiable numbers. ChatGPT-generated ones often aren't.

2. Fake competitor data: fabricated customer counts, usage numbers, or feature sets that sound plausible but don't match the real world. U.S. banks face intense competition from money market funds, a context point ChatGPT might miss or misrepresent. BoursoBank acquired 1.5 million new clients in 2022, a real figure you'd need to verify rather than trust an AI to recall accurately.

3. Misrepresented regulatory timelines: [hallucination of references is well-documented](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09401). In medical decision support tests, [hallucination rates ranged from 50 to 82%](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12318031/) across models. In systematic review accuracy tests, [ChatGPT had many references that didn't exist or didn't support claims](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e53164/).

Data validation is crucial as AI interpretations require human verification. Researchers should verify AI-generated conclusions with expert oversight.

**Verification checklist:**

* Cross-check any key statistic with 2 to 3 independent sources.

* Sanity-check magnitudes: if ChatGPT says "100% adoption," verify externally.

* Confirm critical figures with experts or official filings.

* Check that any cited study or report actually exists and says what ChatGPT attributes.
