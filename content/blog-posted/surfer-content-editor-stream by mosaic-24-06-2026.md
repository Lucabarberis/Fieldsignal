# Stream by Mosaic: Profile, Pricing, and Alternatives

Stream by Mosaic is an expert transcript library used by business and financial professionals to access investor led expert interviews, prep for earnings, and run market checks. AlphaSense acquired Stream to enhance its intelligence platform, and the product now sits inside the broader AlphaSense research suite. If you're evaluating transcript libraries or expert networks for your research workflow, this is what you need to know.

## What Stream by Mosaic is and why it matters for you

Stream (often called "Stream by Mosaic") is one of the main transcript libraries you'll hear about if you work in investment research, corporate development, or strategy consulting. It targets investment analysts and fund managers who need fast access to qualitative expert commentary on public and private companies.

The platform combines a searchable transcript database with the ability to run live expert calls. That puts it in the same category as Tegus and other expert networks that offer both a library and custom sourcing. Financial professionals use it alongside earnings calls, SEC filings, and sell-side research to build conviction before making decisions.

This article covers how Stream works, typical pricing models, when it fits your workflow, how it compares to Mosaic Research Management, Tegus, AlphaSense, and traditional expert networks like GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, Guidepoint, Coleman Research, and others. It also explains why a pay-per-use option like FieldSignal may be more practical for smaller teams, founders, and boutique firms that don't need a six-figure annual subscription.

## Stream by Mosaic: background and product overview

Stream by Mosaic was originally founded by Mosaic Research Management. Stream launched in 2020 and became central to Mosaic's business, serving as the tech enabled market intelligence layer on top of Mosaic's traditional expert network operations. Mosaic historically operated as a classical expert network focused on custom expert calls and compliance. The "Stream by Mosaic" branding reflected Mosaic's role in building and scaling the platform for investment professionals.

Stream is a SaaS platform for qualitative research content. It offers a rapidly growing database of expert interview transcripts, and includes thousands of on-demand transcripts of expert interviews covering wide ranging industries. The platform scaled from hundreds to 10k+ active users over a short period, and Stream launched in 2020 and served 10,000 active analysts by the time it attracted acquisition interest.

AlphaSense [acquired Stream by Mosaic in October 2021](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alphasense-acquires-stream-by-mosaic-delivering-market-intelligence-from-an-extensive-library-of-investor-led-expert-interviews-301407437.html). AlphaSense acquired Stream to enhance market intelligence capabilities and to integrate its research capabilities into the AlphaSense search platform. The transition to AlphaSense had $0 downtime during migration, keeping stream customers on the platform without interruption. AlphaSense's acquisition aimed to integrate research capabilities into its platform, combining stream's content library with AlphaSense's existing document universe of filings, earnings transcripts, broker research, and news.

At the time of the acquisition, the library reportedly held around [8,500 transcripts](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alphasense-acquires-stream-by-mosaic-delivering-market-intelligence-from-an-extensive-library-of-investor-led-expert-interviews-301407437.html). After AlphaSense's later acquisition of Tegus in 2024, the combined expert transcript library grew dramatically. AlphaSense now provides access to over 250,000 investor-led insights through its Expert Transcript Library. That strong revenue growth driven by acquisitions and organic expansion brought AlphaSense past [$500 million ARR](https://fortune.com/2026/04/14/exclusive-alphasense-names-new-cfo-as-revenue-tops-500-million/) by early 2026, with over 6,500 customers.

Here's what a user actually sees on stream's platform:

* A web app with a transcript search bar powered by al search technology

* Filters by company, ticker, sector, expert type (former customer, ex-employee, supplier), and date range

* Transcript previews showing speaker context, financial metrics cited, and qualitative commentary

* Integration with AlphaSense's broader document search, including filings, earnings calls, and broker research

Stream is mainly aimed at long-only and long/short funds, growth equity, and private equity teams that consume a high volume of expert content each month. It's the kind of tool that makes sense when you're reading dozens or hundreds of transcripts a quarter.

## Key use cases for business and financial professionals

Stream is optimized for high-intensity research workflows where transcript volume and speed matter more than hyper-custom targeting. It provides actionable insights for informed decision-making across several common scenarios.

The platform targets investment analysts, fund managers, portfolio managers, and business executives. Expert consultations deliver unique market intelligence from investor-led interviews. Expert insights help identify trends across public and private markets. Stream by Mosaic provides access to interview transcripts for market intelligence across these use cases:

* **Pre-investment work on a public company.** Ahead of earnings season or a rating change, analysts pull transcripts from former customers, channel partners, and ex-employees to check competitive positioning and pricing dynamics.

* **Market mapping for a PE deal.** A fund wants to hear from ex-executives, future customers, and channel partners across 30 to 60 days of diligence. Existing transcripts give a head start before commissioning new calls.

* **Portfolio monitoring.** Ongoing expert calls about competitive moves, pricing shifts, and churn signals help portfolio managers track names they already own.

* **Thematic research across a category.** For example: B2B payments, HR tech, oncology, or semiconductor supply chains. You search for a theme, read across multiple companies, and extract business critical insights from dozens of calls.

* **Vendor or product benchmarking.** Multi-expert views from buyers and ex-sales leaders help benchmark competitors and identify growth opportunities. Expert insights can benchmark competitors across product quality, pricing, and go-to-market execution.

* **Assumption validation.** Expert consultations help validate assumptions and uncover red flags before you commit capital. This is particularly useful during financial analysis of targets in M&A or growth equity.

Knowledge workers use these calls and transcripts alongside 10-Ks, earnings transcripts, sell-side notes, and product demos. Stream's strengths are speed to an already-existing transcript and breadth across thousands of companies. The trade-off: live call depth is constrained by compliance rules and standardized call formats.

## How Stream by Mosaic works in practice

The typical workflow on Stream follows a clear pattern:

1. You search for a company or ticker inside the platform. The search engine, which integrates AI search technology for locating insights, returns relevant transcripts ranked by recency, expert type, and topic match.

2. You read existing investor led expert interviews on the company. Transcripts are usually 45 to 60 minute calls between an invited expert (for example, a former VP of Sales at a SaaS vendor) and an institutional investor. Stream by Mosaic provides flexible access to interviews in text and audio formats.

3. You bookmark relevant transcripts, extract quotes, and build a narrative. The platform offers AI-generated call summaries to highlight relevant business insights, so you can skim quickly before reading the full transcript.

4. If there's a gap in coverage, you request new expert calls on uncovered angles. Such calls are sourced through the network and screened before publication.

Data captured in each transcript includes company names, financial metrics cited by the expert, qualitative commentary on churn, pricing, sales motion, competitive differentiation, and product roadmap expectations. Stream by Mosaic allows content creators to manage research distribution, so the stream team can control how and when transcripts reach the library.

Stream by Mosaic features strict compliance through triple-screened interviews. Experts are screened for conflicts, restricted lists are managed, and material non-public information (MNPI) questions are blocked per standard expert network playbooks. Stream by Mosaic ensures research distribution complies with financial regulations, giving it world class compliance that investment firms relying on the platform expect.

Stream integrates with the broader AlphaSense interface. Users can access stream content directly alongside earnings calls, broker research (for firms with entitlements), and company filings, providing users with a single search platform for multiple content types. AlphaSense's technology enables semantic search, sentiment analysis, and topic extraction across the entire document universe.

## Stream pricing and commercial model

Detailed Stream pricing isn't public. It varies by client size, content entitlements, and whether Stream is bundled within a broader AlphaSense license. Stream's content offering is sold as part of the AlphaSense platform, and pricing depends on the modules you need.

Here are the common patterns used by transcript libraries and expert networks:

* **Annual subscriptions** for on-platform transcript access, tiered by number of seats and total users. Base AlphaSense seats (without expert transcripts) are reported at roughly $10,000 to $15,000 per user per year. With full transcript-library access, estimates run to [$25,000 to $50,000+ per seat per year](https://costbench.com/software/financial-data-terminals/alphasense/) depending on add-ons.

* **Additional per-call or per-project charges** for custom expert calls beyond any included quota. These function similarly to how traditional expert networks charge.

* **Minimum annual commitments** that can run into six-figure ranges for larger hedge funds and multi-strategy platforms. This is standard for investment firms at scale.

These retainers can price out smaller funds, boutique consultancies, and founder-led companies that only need a small number of expert calls or transcripts each quarter. If you're a seed-stage fund or a corporate strategy team doing 4 to 10 projects a year, it's hard to justify locking into a big market subscription.

## Benefits and limitations of Stream by Mosaic

Stream solves specific problems extremely well, but it isn't ideal for every research scope or budget level. It emerged rapidly as an incredibly valuable content platform for high-volume users.

### Benefits

* Instant access to a world class library of prior investor led expert interviews on thousands of companies. Expert consultations provide access to 250,000+ investor-led insights. AlphaSense provides access to over 250,000 investor-led insights, making it one of the broadest single database options for expert interview transcripts.

* Consistent interview format that makes it easy to skim multiple transcripts and build a model of customer sentiment or competitive dynamics. The platform is the market's leading content engine for investor-style expert calls.

* Strong fit for hedge funds, asset management firms, and PE firms that are already comfortable with expert networks like GLG, AlphaSights, and Guidepoint. Top asset management firms and fund managers find stream's library well-suited to their existing workflows.

* Integrated search with other AlphaSense content for clients using that platform for filings and document search. This makes the search platform appealing to diverse user roles across investment teams.

* Stream by Mosaic's broad market footprint across leading companies means you're likely to find relevant coverage for most large-cap and mid-cap public equities.

### Limitations

* Annual subscription and seat-based licensing that can be overkill for a founder, small fund, or corporate strategy team that only runs a handful of projects per year. The fixed cost doesn't scale down well.

* Transcript library may have shallow coverage on niche verticals, early-stage private companies, or geographies outside core focus regions, requiring additional resources from custom sourcing.

* Calls are usually investor led, which can bias question sets toward public market themes rather than operational or product-level detail a corporate buyer might want. Stream's genre of content skews toward what matters for financial analysis, not always product roadmap or customer experience questions.

* Less flexibility if you want to run non-standard formats. For example, structured surveys, panels, or multi-country customer satisfaction studies don't fit stream's content library format.

## When Stream is the right tool vs when to choose an alternative

### Stream is a good fit if

* You work at a hedge fund, long-only manager, or PE shop that runs dozens of expert calls and reads hundreds of transcripts a year. Strong demand for transcript volume justifies the annual cost.

* You're already an AlphaSense client and want expert transcripts accessible in the same search bar as filings and earnings. User adoption is straightforward when you're already on the platform.

* Your investment process relies on seeing consistent interview patterns across many experts on the same name over time. The enriching content in the library lets you track sentiment shifts.

* You need deep market intelligence across a given market with coverage from industry experts, former executives, and customers. Continuous monitoring of customer sentiment can improve satisfaction levels and sharpen portfolio decisions.

### Consider alternatives like FieldSignal if

* You're a founder, corporate strategy lead, or boutique PE/VC investor running fewer, more targeted projects per year. You need even more targeted insights on a specific company or product question, not a broad library.

* You care more about custom expert matching on a narrow ICP, buyer persona, or product niche than you do about transcript volume. The most relevant insights for your project may not exist in any library yet.

* You need a mix of formats. For example, 1:1 expert calls, structured surveys, small panels, and recurring customer satisfaction studies instead of just transcripts.

* You can't justify the annual commitment and want to invest only when you have active projects. Confident decisions don't require a year-round subscription if your research cadence is intermittent.

Many teams pair a transcript-heavy tool (like Stream or Tegus) with a boutique network for more surgical projects. But smaller firms often skip the big subscription entirely and start with pay-per-use.
