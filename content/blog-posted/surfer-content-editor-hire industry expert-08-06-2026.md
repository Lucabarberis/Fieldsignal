### Step 2: Expertise Sourcing and Vetting

FieldSignal identifies and vets relevant industry insiders for your project, using specialized recruiters with deep knowledge of the relevant industry to source the right people. This includes verifying credentials, checking for conflicts of interest, confirming employer restrictions, and ensuring the expert is permitted to discuss the topics in your brief.

You review expert profiles and approve candidates before any live interviews begin. FieldSignal looks beyond inbound applicants and does not rely only on people responding to a job post. You control who you talk to. Avoid experts with biases or excessive opinions on many topics. Look for people who held directly relevant roles recently.

Schedule interviews directly with approved experts. FieldSignal manages logistics, compliance, and recording (where permitted).

Receive transcripts and key insights within 48 hours of completion. You get structured output, not just raw conversation, and insight quality depends on the expert's ability to explain workflows, systems, or day-to-day interaction patterns relevant to your decision so your team can act on the research immediately.

No guesswork. No wasted calls. Just structured progress toward the decision you need to make.
