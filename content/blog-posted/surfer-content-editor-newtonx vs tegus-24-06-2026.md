# NewtonX vs Tegus: Custom Research vs Platform Approach

NewtonX and Tegus solve different research problems. NewtonX custom-recruits experts for each project using AI-powered search tools, delivering primary research through B2B surveys and qualitative interviews. Tegus gives you instant access to a searchable library of pre recorded expert interviews and on-demand expert calls, now integrated into the AlphaSense platform. If you need fresh, statistically significant data from hard-to-find respondents, NewtonX is the better fit. If you need to quickly scan past expert interviews on public companies for due diligence, Tegus wins.

Both NewtonX and Tegus cater to market research needs but with different approaches. Here's how they compare across methodology, expert sourcing, technology, pricing, and use cases.

## NewtonX vs Tegus: Key Differences in the Expert Network Industry

The core distinction is custom sourcing versus platform-based research.

* NewtonX specializes in custom project-based recruiting for high-precision research. It builds expert panels from scratch for each engagement using machine learning and its proprietary Knowledge Graph.

* Tegus focuses on an on-demand library of expert call transcripts. It offers a library with over 200,000 transcripts from expert calls, giving investment professionals and corporate research teams immediate material to work with.

* NewtonX uses AI-driven technology for bespoke expert recruitment, targeting specific demographics, seniority levels, and experience criteria you define.

* Tegus operates on a subscription model providing access to a searchable library, plus the option to schedule live expert calls when transcripts don't cover what you need.

The expert network market reached [$3 billion in 2025](https://expertnetworks.net/networks/newtonx?utm_source=openai), and over 11,200 firms use expert networks globally, a 150% increase since 2022. In the broader expert network landscape, that expansion also reflects how expert network companies focus is shifting toward speed, precision, compliance, and clearer delivery model differences. That growth means more options across expert network platforms for you, but also more confusion about which model fits your research process.

## Market Research Methodology

### NewtonX Custom Research Approach

NewtonX doesn't maintain a standing panel. It recruits fresh for every project.

NewtonX's Knowledge Graph enables custom recruitment from over 1 billion professionals across roughly 140 industries. When you submit research requests, their AI-powered matching algorithms identify relevant experts based on your specific criteria, verify them, and recruit them into your study for targeted expert matching.

This approach supports both qualitative research (expert interviews, AI-moderated conversations) and large-scale quantitative surveys. One published example: NewtonX recruited 18 senior fintech decision-makers across the US and UK for [Coinbase](https://www.newtonx.com/case-study/coinbase-ai-go-to-market/?utm_source=openai) and delivered full qualitative insights in three weeks using AI-moderated interviews. Another project fielded 4,000 verified ad-tech respondents across 13 countries in a quarterly tracker.

NewtonX provides detailed qualitative interviews and quantitative surveys, which makes it more of a research firm than a traditional expert network: its model centers on custom primary work, faster turnaround, and fresh fielded data rather than traditional research built mainly from archived or secondary sources.

### Tegus Platform-Based Research

Tegus takes the opposite approach. Instead of building from scratch, it gives you a massive transcript library to search first. It operates as an expert network provider built around a searchable content platform rather than custom fieldwork.

The [Tegus expert transcript library](https://expertnetworks.net/networks/alphasense-tegus/?utm_source=openai) covers roughly 27,000 companies, with about 8,000 new transcripts added monthly. You search using natural language queries, find past expert interviews relevant to your question, and read or listen immediately.

When transcripts don't cover your topic, you can schedule live expert calls through the platform. Since AlphaSense completed its [$930 million acquisition of Tegus](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alphasense-completes-acquisition-of-tegus-302190934.html?tc=eml_cleartime&utm_source=openai) in July 2024, Tegus also integrates with financial data through Canalyst, company value chain models, and AlphaSense's broader document and filings libraries.

Tegus is ideal for investment professionals needing rapid access to expert insights through past transcripts and live calls on publicly traded companies or well-covered sectors.

## Expert Sourcing and Quality

### NewtonX Expert Identification

NewtonX uses AI for rapid expert identification and verification. Every respondent goes through 100% identity and subject-matter verification for each project. That means both confirming who the person is and confirming they actually have the specialized knowledge you're paying for.

The AI-powered search tools enable instant expert access to niche segments, which is where NewtonX’s value comes from through industry specialization when clients need narrowly defined expertise. If you need former executives from a specific SaaS vertical in Southeast Asia, or senior procurement leaders at mid-market manufacturers, NewtonX's Knowledge Graph scans across its 1.1 billion professional records to find matches.

NewtonX integrates AI-driven compliance protocols into expert matching, reducing the risk of interviewing someone who shouldn't be sharing information on a given topic and supporting robust compliance when sourcing experts for sensitive topics.

### Tegus Expert Network

Tegus maintains a pre-vetted database of former executives and industry professionals as part of a global expert network. Expert quality is maintained through past call performance and client feedback. The network has broad expert coverage in technology, healthcare research, consumer, and financial services sectors, and this model works best when clients can source from existing network depth rather than custom-recruiting each project.

Tegus monitors expert calls for compliance violations. All transcripts go through compliance review before publication. Public company calls are published after a 14-day delay, while private company transcripts follow longer review periods.

The difference: NewtonX gives you control over exactly who you talk to. Tegus gives you access to what industry experts have already said, plus the option to schedule new calls from their existing network.

## Technology Platform and User Experience

### NewtonX Technology Stack

NewtonX's technology platform centers on its Knowledge Graph, which maps expert relationships and identifies optimal candidates for each project. The system handles:

* Automated survey deployment and real-time response monitoring

* AI-powered quality control that flags inconsistent or low-quality responses

* Custom dashboards showing project progress and preliminary results

* Artificial intelligence moderation for qualitative interviews, where AI handles follow-up questions and codes responses in real time

NewtonX also offers synthetic data augmentation (NXAD) for rare segments where sample sizes are extremely limited. This is useful for reaching audiences that traditional expert networks can't fill, though strict compliance regimes may question synthetic augmentation if they require purely primary source data.

### Tegus Platform Features

Tegus built its technology platform around search and discovery across its extensive transcript library. Key features include:

* Advanced AI natural language processing for searching across transcripts

* Self-service expert booking and call scheduling

* Integration with the AlphaSense market intelligence platform since the 2024 acquisition

* Mobile and web access for transcript review and expert call management

Since the acquisition, AlphaSense has pushed integration further: tighter linkage between expert transcripts and filings, AI agents for generative summarization, and "channel check" tools across the Tegus content. For investment firms and asset managers doing ongoing competitive intelligence, having financial data, broker research, and expert transcripts in one place reduces the friction of switching between multiple networks and tools.

## Expert Network Pricing Structure

Pricing is where these two models diverge most sharply.

### NewtonX Pricing Model

NewtonX uses per-project pricing. Costs vary based on:

* Sample size requirements

* Expert seniority and scarcity

* Geographic reach

* Method (quantitative survey vs. qualitative interview vs. AI-moderated)

There are no annual subscription fees or minimum volume commitments. You pay for what you need, when you need it. This is better for consulting firms or private equity firms running one-off studies without wanting to commit to an annual contract.

### Tegus Pricing Approach

Tegus runs on an enterprise subscription model. Annual commitments for expert networks typically range from $20,000 to $500,000, and Tegus seats start at roughly $15,000+ per year depending on transcript access, user seats, and modules.

## Ideal Use Cases

### When NewtonX Works Best

* **Market sizing studies** requiring statistically significant sample sizes across multiple geographies

* **Product development research** needing specific customer segment insights from industry leaders you can't find in a transcript library

* **Competitive intelligence** across hard-to-reach professional groups (niche technical roles, emerging market participants)

* **Pre-launch market validation** with quantitative confidence levels for management consulting firms or corporate strategy teams

* **Global research** needing respondents from specific regions where transcript archives have poor coverage

Expert consultations help validate investment theses and strategies, but when you need fresh data from people who haven't already been interviewed by your competitors, NewtonX's custom sourcing approach gives you an information edge.

### When Tegus Excels

* **Investment due diligence** requiring immediate access to company-specific insights and market trends

* **Ongoing market monitoring** through transcript library searches for asset managers and analyst teams

* **Quick analyst led interviews** on publicly traded companies with broad industry coverage

* **Research teams** needing both historical insights and fresh expert perspectives in one platform

* **Healthcare research, life sciences**, and financial services where Tegus has deep sector coverage

Expert networks help validate market entry strategies through direct insights, and Tegus's archive of prior expert interviews gives you a head start before scheduling a live call. Investment firms use expert networks for due diligence in market entry, and Tegus's integration with financial data and filings makes it particularly strong for this use case.

## Compliance and Risk Management

Both platforms take compliance and risk management seriously, but through different mechanisms.

NewtonX is certified with SOC 2 and complies with ESOMAR, Insights Association, MRS, and BBB Data Privacy Framework standards. Its strict compliance protocols are baked into the expert matching process itself, with 100% verification of identity and subject-matter relevance for every respondent.

Tegus under AlphaSense inherits AlphaSense's compliance infrastructure. Tegus monitors expert calls for compliance violations, and all transcripts go through review before publication. The delay system (14 days for public companies, longer for private) is designed to reduce MNPI risk.

GLG trains experts on handling sensitive information. AlphaSights uses compliance monitoring to check expert calls. Guidepoint vets all experts before they join the network. Across the expert network industry, compliance equivalence is the baseline expectation. The question is whether the specific model (fresh recruitment vs. published transcripts) creates different risk profiles for your use case.

Organizations conducting 20+ consultations annually benefit from systematic evaluations of their expert network provider's compliance framework. Expert networks provide rapid access to specialized knowledge for informed decisions, but that access needs to come with proper guardrails.
