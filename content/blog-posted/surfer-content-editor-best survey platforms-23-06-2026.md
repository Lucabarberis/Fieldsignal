# Best Survey Platforms for B2B Research: 10 Compared

If you're running market research, competitive intelligence, or customer satisfaction studies for a B2B audience, the survey platform you pick matters more than most people think. Not all tools are built for the rigor B2B research demands. Some are designed for quick consumer polls. Others can handle conjoint analysis, quota controls, and enterprise compliance, but cost six figures a year.

This article compares 10 survey platforms on criteria that actually matter for B2B research teams. We cover what each tool does well, where it falls short, and which type of buyer it fits. We also flag the gap that most survey software can't fill on its own: reaching verified senior decision makers and subject-matter experts for qualitative insight.

The platforms reviewed here range from completely free tools to enterprise-grade research suites. Not all tools on this list are equal, and that's the point. Some handle basic data collection. Others support advanced research methodologies. You need to know which is which before you commit budget.

## How We Chose the Best Survey Platforms for B2B Research

We evaluated each platform against seven criteria that B2B research teams care about most. These aren't theoretical. They come from real friction points that strategy, product, and market research teams hit when running surveys against business audiences.

**Data quality controls and fraud detection.** B2B decisions rely on input from verified professionals. Cheap panels with weak identity checks produce unreliable data. We looked at what each platform does to screen respondents and detect bots. Many panels claim B2B coverage but are [consumer-profiled with self-reported job titles](https://www.emporiaresearch.com/blog/consumer-profiled-vs-true-b2b-profiled-panels-understanding-the-hidden-difference-in-data-quality), which degrades precision.

**Audience panel access and targeting.** Can the platform reach your target respondents, or do you need to bring your own list? For B2B, this is often the hardest part. Access to niche audiences (CTOs in biotech, procurement leads in manufacturing) determines whether your study is feasible.

**Advanced question types and research methodologies.** Conjoint analysis, MaxDiff, experimental design, monadic concept testing. These methods separate a survey maker from a research platform. If you need to test pricing trade-offs or feature prioritization, you need these.

**Integration capabilities.** Does the tool connect to your CRM, BI tools, or statistical software? Export to SPSS, R, or CSV matters for analysis. Salesforce and HubSpot integrations matter for automated follow up workflows from survey responses. Microsoft Forms integrates seamlessly with Microsoft 365 applications, and GetFeedback integrates directly with Salesforce for customer feedback, but both are narrow in scope.

**Pricing transparency.** Hidden costs on quotas, screening, translations, and data export are common. We flagged platforms where the sticker price doesn't match total cost of ownership. Some tools like SurveyPlanet and Sogolytics provide unlimited surveys and responses in their free plan, making them worth noting for teams watching budget.

**Compliance and data security.** Enterprise research requires SOC2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, or FedRAMP certifications. SmartSurvey, for example, is GDPR-compliant and UK-hosted. We checked what each platform actually certifies, not just what their marketing pages imply.

**Speed of data collection.** If your research window is two weeks before a board meeting or investment committee, turnaround time matters as much as methodology.

## Best 10 Survey Platforms for B2B Research

### 1. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is the enterprise standard for survey creation and experience management. It's built for organizations that need research-grade capabilities: complex survey logic, advanced statistical analysis, text analytics, and predictive modeling. Qualtrics is used by over 13,000 brands globally for research, and it's recognized for advanced academic and enterprise research.

#### Why It Stands Out

Qualtrics supports advanced statistical analysis and text analytics at a level that most online survey tools can't match. Its Stats iQ module lets you run regression, pivot tables, and correlation analysis inside the platform. For market researchers who need conjoint analysis, MaxDiff, or complex quota management, this is where Qualtrics earns its price tag.

#### Best For

Enterprise strategy teams running large-scale market research. Academic research institutions. Corporate development teams that need methodological rigor and audit-ready data. If your organization already has a Qualtrics license, it's the obvious choice for serious B2B studies.

#### Key Strengths

* Advanced analytics including conjoint analysis, MaxDiff, and experimental design

* Qualtrics meets GDPR, HIPAA, and FedRAMP compliance standards

* Sophisticated survey logic with branching, piping, and quota controls

* Multichannel deployment (email, web, SMS, in-app)

* Strong data export to SPSS, R, CSV, and other statistical formats

#### Possible Limitations

* Qualtrics pricing starts above $1,500 per year for meaningful functionality. [Median enterprise annual spend is roughly $27,600](https://www.clearlyrated.com/blog/qualtrics-vs-surveymonkey). Many features require add-on purchases.

* Steep learning curve. Most teams need formal training before they can use it effectively.

* Qualtrics offers a free plan with 500 total responses allowed, but it's too limited for real research.

* Overkill for simple customer feedback or internal surveys.

### 2. Pollfish

Pollfish is an audience-powered online survey platform with a built-in consumer panel. It skips the traditional panel recruitment process by reaching respondents through mobile apps and websites. Pollfish connects to a network of 250 million consumers globally.

#### Why It Stands Out

Speed. Pollfish can gather 500+ responses within hours for broad topics. For teams that don't have an existing panel or contact list, this removes the biggest bottleneck in field research. The platform also takes data quality seriously. Pollfish has a 40-person data quality team and a 40-person Security and Compliance Department. They use a 14-day evaluation period for new panelists before allowing them into active studies.

#### Best For

Startups doing market validation against consumer audiences. Product teams that need fast directional data. Researchers without existing panels who need survey responses quickly. It's a strong survey app for mobile-first respondent access.

#### Key Strengths

* Built-in global consumer audience, no recruitment needed

* Fast turnaround, often same-day results

* Pollfish charges $0.95 per response for basic targeting, making costs predictable

* Pollfish offers 16 question types for surveys

* Strong fraud detection and respondent vetting protocols

#### Possible Limitations

* The panel is consumer-focused. If you need C-level B2B decision makers or niche industry experts, Pollfish won't reliably reach them. This is a critical distinction for B2B research.

* Survey design capabilities are limited compared to dedicated survey platforms like Qualtrics or Alchemer.

* Advanced features like conjoint or MaxDiff are not available.

* Response limits on cheaper tiers can add up when you factor in screening for low-incidence audiences.

### 3. SurveyMonkey

SurveyMonkey is known for broad, all-around utility in surveys. It's the most recognized name in online survey tools, and that brand recognition carries weight with stakeholders who need to trust the data source. SurveyMonkey is recognized for its reliability in survey software.

#### Why It Stands Out

Trust and familiarity. When you share a survey link from SurveyMonkey, recipients know what it is. That matters for completion rates in B2B contexts where respondents are skeptical of unknown tools. SurveyMonkey offers over 200 templates and deep integrations, covering everything from employee surveys to customer satisfaction tracking.

#### Best For

Mid-market companies running customer feedback programs, collecting feedback from partners, or conducting employee surveys. Teams that need a reliable, broadly accepted tool for basic plan research without a long onboarding process.

#### Key Strengths

* Extensive template library covering common B2B research use cases

* SurveyMonkey has over 200 native integrations with various tools, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and Tableau

* Audience panel access through partnerships for external research

* Simple survey creation with minimal learning curve

#### Possible Limitations

* SurveyMonkey's free plan limits responses to 25 per survey, which makes the free survey option impractical for real research.

* Advanced analytics, sentiment analysis, and research methodologies like conjoint require expensive upgrades to paid plans.

* Per-user pricing can get costly. Teams of five or more will feel it.

* Survey logic is limited compared to research-grade platforms.

### 4. Alchemer

Alchemer (formerly SurveyGizmo) sits between SurveyMonkey and Qualtrics in both capability and price. It's built for B2B teams that need deep CRM integrations and workflow automation without Qualtrics-level cost.

#### Why It Stands Out

Alchemer's strength is connecting survey data directly into your business systems. Its native Salesforce and HubSpot integrations are deeper than what most survey tools offer. Alchemer offers advanced API connections and webhooks, letting you trigger actions in other systems based on survey responses.

#### Best For

B2B companies with complex CRM workflows. Customer experience teams that need to route feedback into ticketing or account management systems. Teams that need enterprise research capabilities at a lower price point than Qualtrics.

#### Key Strengths

* Deep native Salesforce and HubSpot integrations

* Automated follow-up workflows and data routing based on responses

* Alchemer provides HIPAA compliance options for sensitive data

* SOC2 certified, EU data residency options

* Wide range of question types supporting A/B design and quotas

#### Possible Limitations

* [Pricing ranges from $165 to $275 per user per month](https://freesurveymakers.com/alchemer/) depending on tier. Enterprise plans are custom-quoted.

* The interface feels dated compared to modern tools like Typeform or SurveySparrow.

* No free plan or free survey software option. You're paying from day one.

* Smaller brand presence means less stakeholder recognition.

### 5. QuestionPro

QuestionPro offers enterprise research capabilities at mid-market pricing. Its Research Suite includes conjoint analysis, MaxDiff, and A/B testing, features that usually require Qualtrics-level spend.

#### Why It Stands Out

The ratio of advanced features to cost is QuestionPro's main argument. You get 350+ question types, including methods used in academic research and professional market research, at a fraction of what Qualtrics charges. The platform also provides panel access to over 22 million respondents through its network.

#### Best For

Academic researchers who need advanced skip logic and experimental design. Mid-market companies that want research-grade survey capabilities without enterprise-grade budgets. Teams doing product roadmap validation or pricing studies.

#### Key Strengths

* 350+ question types including conjoint analysis and MaxDiff

* Advanced statistical analysis tools built into the platform

* [Advanced tier pricing at $99/user/month](https://www.questionpro.com/pricing/qp-index.html), billed annually

* Panel access for external audience research

* Data export in multiple formats for offline analysis

#### Possible Limitations

* The interface is less intuitive than leading competitors. There's a learning curve, especially for the advanced research suite.

* Integration ecosystem is smaller than SurveyMonkey or Qualtrics.

* Free and basic tiers have strict response limits.

* Sample quality through built-in panels may not be sufficient for niche B2B expert audiences.

### 6. Typeform

Typeform is distinguished by its conversational interface for high engagement. It's a form builder and survey maker designed around visual design and user experience, not research methodology.

#### Why It Stands Out

Typeform enhances user experience with its visual design and interface. The one-question-at-a-time format enhances user engagement and reduces drop-off. For teams where completion rates are the priority, this design approach works. Typeform's one-question-at-a-time format is particularly effective for lead qualification, onboarding flows, and branded survey experiences.

#### Best For

Marketing teams running lead qualification surveys. Customer onboarding research. Teams that need high response rates from a pop up forms or embedded survey experience. Anyone who values custom branding and design polish.

#### Key Strengths

* High completion rates driven by conversational, visual design

* Strong for lead generation and marketing-adjacent research

* Clean integrations with marketing automation tools

* Ability to create unlimited forms on paid plans with logic jumps and hidden fields

* Excellent for branded survey experiences

#### Possible Limitations

* Typeform's free plan allows 10 responses per month. That's a generous free plan in name only, but it's nearly useless for real data collection.

* Advanced analytics for complex research are limited compared to Qualtrics or QuestionPro.

* No native conjoint, MaxDiff, or experimental design tools.

* Higher cost per response for large-scale studies since pricing is subscription-based regardless of volume.

### 7. LimeSurvey

LimeSurvey is an open-source survey platform that lets you self-host on your own infrastructure. It's been around [since 2003](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LimeSurvey) (originally called PHPSurveyor) and is widely used in academic, government, and institutional research where data sovereignty is non-negotiable.

#### Why It Stands Out

Complete data control. When you self-host LimeSurvey, your survey data never touches a third-party server. LimeSurvey offers GDPR compliance for self-hosted solutions because you control the entire stack. For organizations in regulated industries, this is the deciding factor.

#### Best For

Government agencies, research institutions, and healthcare organizations that require data sovereignty. Teams in the EU or other jurisdictions with strict data residency requirements. Organizations with in-house IT that can manage hosting and maintenance.

#### Key Strengths

* No response limits when self-hosted. You can create unlimited surveys with no per-response fees.

* Supports 80+ languages, making it strong for multinational studies

* Flexible question types with branching logic and quotas

* Data export to SPSS, R, CSV, and other statistical formats

* Completely free as open-source software (cloud plans also available)

#### Possible Limitations

* Requires technical expertise for setup, hosting, and maintenance. This isn't a click-and-go tool.

* No built-in audience panel. You must bring your own contacts.

* The interface and visual polish are limited compared to commercial platforms.

* Integrations are less developed. Connecting to CRMs or BI tools takes custom work.

### 8. SurveySparrow

SurveySparrow is built around conversational surveys and recurring feedback programs. SurveySparrow's chat-like interface improves survey completion rates by making surveys feel less like forms and more like conversations.

#### Why It Stands Out

The conversational format is the differentiator. SurveySparrow reports up to 40% higher completion rates compared to traditional survey formats. For B2B teams running recurring NPS, customer satisfaction, or employee pulse programs, this engagement boost compounds over time. The platform also includes 1,500+ integrations and built-in ticket management for closing the feedback loop.

#### Best For

Customer success teams running ongoing feedback programs. HR teams managing employee surveys. B2B companies that need recurring, automated survey collection with follow-up capability.

#### Key Strengths

* Conversational interface that drives higher response rates

* Strong automation for recurring surveys with scheduled distribution

* [SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and GDPR certified](https://surveysparrow.com/legal/security/) with AES-256 encryption

* Built-in ticket management for acting on feedback

* White-labeling and sub-account management for agencies

#### Possible Limitations

* Advanced research methodologies like conjoint analysis or MaxDiff aren't available. Not built for advanced research projects.

* The integration ecosystem, while large, is limited compared to SurveyMonkey's depth.

* Advanced features require enterprise-tier pricing.

* Panel access for external B2B audiences is not a primary strength.

### 9. Suzy

Suzy combines a verified consumer panel with mixed quantitative and qualitative research tools. It's designed for brand and product teams that need fast, reliable consumer insights with professional analysis included.

#### Why It Stands Out

Speed and quality assurance. Suzy delivers insights in hours, not weeks. The platform uses its proprietary "Biotic" protocol for bot detection and identity verification, setting a higher bar for data quality than most panel providers. [Suzy's pricing model charges per question rather than per respondent](https://www.suzy.com/blog/stretching-research-budgets), which helps with budget predictability, especially for low-incidence audiences.

#### Best For

Brand teams running concept, pricing, or packaging tests. Product managers who need consumer benchmarking data. Competitive benchmarking projects that require fast turnaround with verified respondents.

#### Key Strengths

* Rapid turnaround with insights often delivered same-day

* Verified, high-quality consumer panel with identity checks

* Professional analysis and reporting included

* Per-question pricing reduces cost surprises for niche targeting

* Supports both quantitative surveys and qualitative open-text analysis

#### Possible Limitations

* High cost, starting at $30,000+ per year. This isn't for small teams or one-off studies.

* Primarily US-focused audience panel. Global coverage is more limited.

* The panel skews consumer. It's not built for reaching senior B2B decision makers, C-level executives, or niche industry experts.

* AI powered survey creation and design tools are less flexible than dedicated survey platforms.

For internal surveys, quick polls, and basic data collection, it's the best free survey tool available. ## How to Choose the Right Survey Platform for B2B Research

Effective online research depends on specific user needs. There's no single best free survey software or paid platform that fits every scenario. Here's how to narrow it down.

### Choose Based on Research Scope and Audience

Start with who you're trying to reach and what you're trying to learn.

* **Internal surveys** (employee engagement, team pulse): Google Forms, SurveyMonkey, or SurveySparrow. Keep it simple.

* **Consumer research** (concept testing, brand perception): Pollfish or Suzy. You need their panels.

* **B2B decision-maker research** (market validation, competitive intel, pricing studies): This is where most survey platforms fall short. Consumer panels with self-reported job titles don't reliably reach verified executives. You'll likely need to combine a survey platform with a specialized panel or expert network.

* **Academic research** requiring experimental design: Qualtrics or QuestionPro.

### Choose Based on Budget and Pricing Model

Your budget determines the tier you can access, and the tier determines whether you get ai powered survey creation, advanced features, or just basic survey capabilities.

* **Zero budget**: Google Forms for basics. Qualtrics free plan gives you 500 total responses if you need slightly more structure.

* **Under $200/month**: QuestionPro Advanced or SurveyMonkey Team plans. Good enough for mid-market research.

* **$200-500/month per user**: Alchemer or QuestionPro Research Suite. This is where you get CRM integration and advanced methodologies.

* **$1,500+/year and up**: Qualtrics. Full enterprise stack.

* **$30,000+/year**: Suzy. Panel access with analysis included.

Watch for hidden costs. Quotas, screening, translations, and premium data export formats often aren't included in base pricing.

### Choose Based on Integration and Workflow Needs

If you live in Salesforce, Alchemer or GetFeedback make sense. If your team uses Google Sheets as a primary data tool, Google Forms or tools with Google Sheets integration are the path of least resistance. If you need ai survey creation, check whether it's included or an add-on.

The support team responsiveness also matters. Enterprise tools generally provide dedicated support. Free survey tools and basic plan tiers typically offer community forums or email-only support.
