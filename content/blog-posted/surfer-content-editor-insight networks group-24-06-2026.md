## Limitations and Trade-offs vs Larger Expert Networks

Every expert network involves trade-offs between breadth, price, and process control. ING is no exception.

* **Speed and breadth.** ING's curated model can limit how fast you get your first expert match. Large networks maintain huge searchable databases and 24/7 sourcing teams that deliver faster responses.

* **Global coverage.** If you need 30+ markets in parallel, GLG, Guidepoint, or Third Bridge are a better operational fit. Guidepoint operates with over one million expert profiles across 200+ industries.

* **Pricing opacity.** Like many boutiques, detailed rate structures and markups aren't always visible upfront. That makes budget forecasting harder for junior associates.

* **Technology.** ING doesn't promote large-scale transcript libraries, AI search, or machine learning tools for analysis. Expert networks provide access to transcript libraries of prior interviews, but ING's research remains more manual. Tegus, for comparison, has over 200,000 investor-led expert call transcripts.

This isn't inherently negative. Match ING to project types where white-glove, low-volume insight beats automation and scale.
