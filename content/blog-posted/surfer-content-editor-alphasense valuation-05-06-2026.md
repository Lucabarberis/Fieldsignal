## How investors infer AlphaSense’s valuation

Private tech companies are typically valued at a multiple of their Annual Recurring Revenue. Annual Recurring Revenue is a primary baseline for the valuation of subscription-based SaaS companies, so annual recurring revenue matters more than one-off sales.

Investors usually triangulate AlphaSense valuation through:

1. Primary round pricing, where new preferred shares set the post-money valuation.

2. Public reports from funding news, company press, and venture databases.

3. Fund marks from long term investors that hold private shares.

4. Secondary trades, when buyers and sellers reset price expectations.

5. Revenue quality, churn, gross margin, and the ability to renew enterprise contracts.

6. Competition in the SaaS sector, which affects multiples and discount rates.
