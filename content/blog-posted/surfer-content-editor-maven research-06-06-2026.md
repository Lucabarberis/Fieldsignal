## Maven Research: company background and business model

Maven Research launched in 2008 in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, with the aim of making specialized knowledge accessible on demand. The company was co-founded by Wyatt Nordstrom (Chairman) and Mark Platosh (CEO/CTO). Their vision was to create a two-sided marketplace for specialized expertise, coining the term "microconsulting" to describe short-duration expert engagements rather than traditional long advisory retainers.

Maven operates as a two-sided marketplace for specialized knowledge. Organizations can access over [500,000 vetted professionals](https://www.maven.co/expert-marketplace) through the platform, and Maven's network also includes over 11,000 professionals globally in its active consulting pool. The company utilizes a crowdsourced referral system to connect clients with experts, and its platform accelerates decision-making using machine learning for matching. Maven verifies B2B samples by actively vetting professional credentials.

Experts join Maven to share knowledge on a flexible gig-work basis. Maven's network includes thousands of industry professionals and thought leaders across sectors like healthcare, industrials, SaaS, and emerging markets. Revenue comes from call fees, project fees, and subscription packages. Clients include corporations, consulting firms, PE/VC funds, and business angels looking for direct access to specialized expertise.

## Consulting on Maven Research: expert experience and payment issues

Maven onboards experts through outreach, profile creation, screening questions, compliance training, and rate setting. A typical engagement involves a 45 to 60 minute call where the client focuses on specific questions about companies, technologies, or go-to-market strategies. Experts can earn $100 to $500 per hour for consultations, depending on seniority and topic complexity. ## How to choose the right expert network for your next project

Use this decision framework:

1. **Budget flexibility.** If you can't commit to high annual fees, prefer platforms with pay-per-use or project-based pricing. Maven and FieldSignal both work here. FieldSignal wins on transparency.

2. **Urgency.** If you need a call this week, focus on networks that match within 24 hours. Maven and FieldSignal both support this.

3. **Compliance requirements.** If you're a regulated firm or involved in public markets, confirm the network's conflict-of-interest screening, privacy controls, and vetting process before you create an account.

4. **Niche expertise depth.** For deep domain knowledge from former operators, not just thought leaders, ask providers how they source and vet experts.

5. **Pricing transparency.** Ask vendors directly: what's the total cost per call, including expert honoraria? Are there setup fees, cancellation fees, or hidden markups?

Scope your project brief clearly: industry, target companies, core questions, geographies, and required seniority levels. Send it to two or three providers and compare responses on speed, expert quality, and total cost.
