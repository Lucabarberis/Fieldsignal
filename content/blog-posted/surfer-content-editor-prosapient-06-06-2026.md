# ProSapient: Expert Network Profile, Services, and Reviews

## A London-Based Expert Network Built for Institutional Investors

ProSapient is an AI-powered expert network founded in 2017 by Margo Polishchuk and Jordan Shlosberg. It connects clients with industry experts for paid consultations, surveys, and primary research. The company launched its AI-powered platform in 2017 and has expanded rapidly since, positioning itself as a technology-first alternative in an expert network industry that generates close to $2 billion annually, with over 100 expert network companies competing for share.

ProSapient raised $10 million in Series A funding in 2021, led by Smedvig Capital. Before that round, ProSapient achieved 100% year-over-year revenue growth. The company employs over 270 people across four global locations: London, Raleigh, Toronto, and Lisbon.

The company has earned recognition as a Future Fifty company by Tech Nation, was featured among the Financial Times FT1000 fastest-growing businesses in Europe, and was recognized as one of the 100 fastest-growing businesses in Europe. Its solutions focus on connecting clients in private equity, venture capital, and consulting with industry leading experts faster than traditional relationship-driven networks.

If you're a PE associate, strategy analyst, or consultant evaluating expert networks, here's what you need to know about ProSapient's services, how they work, what clients and experts say, and how they compare to alternatives.

## What ProSapient Offers as a Primary Research Platform

ProSapient operates as a primary research platform with five core service lines:

* **Expert consultations.** 1-on-1 or group calls with vetted industry experts. ProSapient connects clients with experts for consultations, and a consultation typically lasts for one hour. Clients can access insights through phone calls and surveys.

* **AI-powered search and matching.** Their "SearchAI" technology uses machine learning, semantic search, entity extraction, and contextual enrichment as **cutting edge technology** to match project requests to relevant experts. It goes beyond keyword matching to interpret title synonyms, company name variants, and context, helping clients make **great decisions**. The system learns from past matches to improve accuracy over time.

* **Survey capabilities.** ProSapient offers AI-moderated interviews for survey projects. You can run online surveys and expert panel questionnaires using their network or broader respondent sourcing. They support survey design, deployment, and results review.

* **Transcript library.** After each expert call, transcripts are produced with claimed 99% accuracy, typically available within four hours. The transcript library is searchable and shareable. AI summaries and translations into 48 languages are included.

* **Compliance and expert vetting.** ProSapient is ISO 27001 accredited for information security. Experts complete mandatory compliance training. Transcripts pass an MNPI (material non-public information) review. The platform is GDPR compliant.

The platform supports custom recruiting for niche audiences, which helps when you need a given expert for specialized **business** needs in fields that standard databases don't cover well.

## How ProSapient Works

### For Clients Seeking Insights

The process is straightforward:

1. **Submit a project request.** You describe the subject matter, geography, seniority level, and any specific criteria. This goes to ProSapient's client services associates, who assist with refining request criteria before outreach begins.

2. **AI-enhanced matching.** SearchAI proposes expert matches from their database of approximately 50,000 experts. The ai powered search identifies candidates even when titles or company names are non-standard.

3. **Vetting and compliance screening.** Human associates further screen whether each expert is a good fit. Experts undergo a vetting call before client proposals. Compliance checks are run before any conversation happens.

4. **Schedule and conduct the call.** Calls are scheduled via the platform with calendar integrations and dial-in options (phone, Zoom, browser). You pick a suitable time, and the team can assist with coordination and logistics as the client call proceeds.

Turnaround from project submission to expert match can happen in hours rather than days, depending on the request specificity, helping clients maintain focus on time-sensitive research.

### For Industry Experts

1. **Recruitment and onboarding.** Experts are recruited proactively or through inbound inquiry. Their profile and history are reviewed for relevance.

2. **Vetting and compliance.** Screening calls or emails assess relevance to specific projects, and experts are encouraged to **respond** promptly to outreach or scheduling messages. If selected, mandatory compliance training is required before any engagement.

3. **Project matching.** Once approved, experts are presented to clients. If the client confirms a suitable fit, the consultation is scheduled. Experts can negotiate their consultation rates based on seniority and expertise.

4. **Payment.** Payment is processed within 45 days after the consultation. Rates are agreed per project. High seniority or niche expertise typically commands higher rates.

Experts who share insights through ProSapient maintain ongoing profiles that may surface for future projects should an **opportunity arise** where their expertise is needed again.
