## Expert Network Support for Financial Due Diligence Without Six-Figure Retainers

Financial due diligence is the process of testing a target company’s financial health, accounting practices, and risks before an investment, acquisition, partnership, carve out, or sell side process. It reviews financial statements, historical financial records, profitability, cash flow, quality of earnings, accounting policies, working capital requirements, and the assumptions behind the business plan.

Good diligence work goes beyond the model. It adds primary research from people who know the business: former employees, customers, suppliers, controllers, CFOs, competitors, and industry insiders.

If you're a private equity associate, VC investor, corporate M&A analyst, boutique consultant, or founder doing pre-fundraise research, you need answers fast. You don't always need a global professional services firm, a large consulting firm, or a global leader with a full transactions practice under one roof.

You need focused diligence services that help you answer:

* Is the target company’s revenue real, recurring, and recognized correctly?

* Are earnings sustainable after normalization adjustments?

* Does working capital change by season, customer type, or geography?

* Are there off-balance sheet liabilities or operational risk the seller hasn’t highlighted?

* Do customers, suppliers, and former finance leaders confirm the financial story?

Traditional networks such as GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, Guidepoint, Tegus, AlphaSense, Capvision, ProSapient, Coleman Research, Atheneum, Mosaic Research Management, and Inex One all serve parts of this market. The issue is access, diligence cost, pricing opacity, and fit for mid market deal size.
