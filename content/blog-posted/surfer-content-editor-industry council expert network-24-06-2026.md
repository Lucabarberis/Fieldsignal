An industry council expert network is a curated panel of senior operators, former executives, and functional leaders you hire to answer specific questions about a sector, company, or deal. It's not a trade association. It's not a lobby group. It's a paid research service that gives you fast, compliant access to people who've worked inside the value chain you're studying.

This model has grown rapidly since around 2010 alongside GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, and Guidepoint. The expert network industry reached roughly $3 billion by 2025, growing about 12% annually. ## What an industry council expert network actually is

An expert network is an intermediary service connecting businesses with subject-matter experts. Expert networks operate as curated matchmaking platforms for insights and experience, covering over 5,000 sectors. Expert networks facilitate client access to vetted industry professionals with niche knowledge. Consultants manually vet experts for quality assurance, unlike open-signup marketplaces.

* Typical profiles: ex-CRO of a regional events company, former VP Sales at a hotel chain, senior product manager at a SaaS registration platform, procurement lead at a Fortune 100 buyer of business events

* Expert consultations primarily include short-term paid interactions such as phone calls, multi-expert panel calls, structured surveys, and advisory retainers

* Many expert networks focus on niche areas where rapid understanding is essential

## How industry council expert networks work step by step

The network maintains a global database of specialists who are rigorously screened. Here's the process:

1. You submit a scope: sector, region, company list, questions.

2. The network builds a mini "council" of 5 to 30 experts.

3. Compliance checks: MNPI screening, conflict-of-interest declarations, cooling-off periods.

4. You review anonymized profiles and select experts.

5. Calls happen. Expert consultations can provide insights within 48 hours.

6. Deliverables: transcripts, an executive summary, and a risk/opportunity grid.

Expert networks handle logistics for consultations to ensure compliance with regulations. Your team, whether junior associates or founders, works with a project manager who refines question guides and manages scheduling.
