## How GLG Insights Works

Getting research through GLG is structured, service-heavy, and designed for enterprise clients.

### Step 1: Project Scoping

You define the research objective with a GLG associate. That might include due diligence on a target company, market sizing, competitive analysis, customer sentiment, product validation, pricing research, or regulatory questions.
