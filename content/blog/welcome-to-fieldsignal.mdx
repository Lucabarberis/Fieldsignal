# Welcome to FieldSignal

This is the first post on the FieldSignal blog. We will publish weekly on:

- Primary research methods that actually work
- How the expert network industry is shifting
- Sector intelligence drawn from our 50,000-strong network
- Buyer guides for teams choosing between GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, Tegus, Guidepoint, and us

## Why bother writing

Most expert network marketing is forgettable. "Best-in-class compliance you can trust." "Robust vetting ensures unparalleled quality." None of it helps a Series A founder, a small hedge fund, or a corporate strategy team decide whether to spend $500 on a call.

We're going to write the opposite: short pieces with **specific numbers**, naming names, telling you when a competitor is actually the right answer.

## What you can expect

> Weekly cadence. 800–1,500 words per post. No fluff, no hype words. One concrete number per section.

The first 8 posts will cover the questions buyers ask us most often:

1. How is FieldSignal different from GLG?
2. What does an expert call actually cost?
3. When does a transcript library beat a custom call?
4. How do compliance frameworks actually differ between networks?

Subscribe to the newsletter if you want these as they ship.
