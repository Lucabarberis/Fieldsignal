## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you get the same expert quality without paying GLG prices?

Yes. Expert quality is driven by how well the network matches your specific research question, how recently the expert was active in the relevant role, and how rigorous the vetting process is. GLG's scale means they have more experts in their database. But most research projects don't need a million options. They need three to five well-matched professionals. FieldSignal and other mid-tier networks recruit from the same talent pools and apply comparable screening. The difference is in pricing structure, not expert caliber. You pay more at GLG for brand, concierge service, and volume infrastructure, not necessarily for better individual experts on your specific call.

### What compliance risks should mid-market teams worry about?

Three things matter most. First, MNPI exposure: if an expert shares material non-public information and you trade on it, you're liable. Any network you use should screen for this before every call. Second, cooling-off periods: experts who recently left a company under discussion shouldn't be on the call. Verify that the network enforces minimum time gaps. Third, audit trails: if a regulator asks what happened on a call two years ago, you need transcripts, attestations, and conflict check records. Ask any provider about their audit trail retention. FieldSignal maintains a multi-year audit trail. If a provider can't clearly explain their compliance process, don't use them.

### How long does expert recruitment typically take?

For common profiles (tech product managers, SaaS sales leaders, healthcare operations), 24–72 hours from brief submission to expert shortlist is standard across GLG, AlphaSights, Guidepoint, and FieldSignal. The call itself typically happens within a week of initial contact. For rare profiles (niche regulatory specialists, highly specific technical engineers, executives in minor geographies), expect 5–10 business days or longer. Factors that speed things up: a clear brief with specific experience requirements, flexibility on scheduling, and willingness to start with transcript content while live calls are being arranged.
